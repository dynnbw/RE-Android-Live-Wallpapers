## Wallpaper images

The images under `app/src/main/assets/` come from the legacy live wallpaper
implementations and sample projects listed below, unless a wallpaper's own `NOTICE.md`
says otherwise. Copyright remains with the respective owners; the Apache-2.0 license of
this repository does not extend to them.

Some wallpapers re-created part or all of their artwork for this project: fall, wildworld,
windmill and earth in full; fireworks, galaxy, galaxy4 and grass in part.

The rendering logic here is a re-implementation, provided for preservation and to keep
these wallpapers running on current Android versions. There is no affiliation with, or
endorsement by, any rights holder. Every wallpaper directory carries a `NOTICE.md`
describing its image source. If you are a rights holder and object to the inclusion of any
asset, please open an issue and it will be removed.
